About Trip Wallet

Managing finances across friends and group travelers should be effortless, clear, and fair. Trip Wallet replaces messy spreadsheets and retroactive split requests with a proactive shared pool.

👥

Collaborative Group Budgeting

Instead of one person bearing large upfront expenses (like villas, transport, or groceries) and chasing people down weeks later, Trip Wallet allows everyone to pool funds into a shared trip wallet before or during the journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Trip Wallet features, payments, and settlements.

What is Trip Wallet?
Trip Wallet is a specialized group finance and shared digital wallet application designed for group trips, tours, and shared vacations. It allows trip members to pool money together into a common trip purse and empowers the trip admin to track expenses, manage splits, and settle balances transparently.
How does adding money to the trip wallet work?
Any member invited to a trip can contribute funds. When you enter an amount to contribute, the app creates a secure payment order through our backend and launches the Razorpay payment checkout. You can pay using UPI, cards, or net banking. Once Razorpay confirms the transaction and our backend cryptographically verifies the signature, your contribution is credited to the shared wallet balance immediately.
What happens if a payment fails?
If a payment fails, is canceled by the user, or encounters a network interruption, no funds are added to the trip wallet. If money is deducted from your bank account during a failed attempt, it is typically reversed automatically by your issuing bank or Razorpay within standard banking timelines (usually 5 to 7 business days).
How are expenses split among trip members?
Trip Wallet offers flexible expense splits. When recording a group payment, you can split the cost equally among all trip participants, assign custom amounts, or exclude members who did not take part in a specific activity. The app automatically computes who owes what against their initial contributions.
How is the remaining wallet balance handled at the end of the trip?
When all expenses are logged and the trip is closed by the admin, Trip Wallet calculates each member's final balance based on their total contributions minus their total share of expenses. The final settlement summary specifies the exact refund or payout amounts due to each member according to Trip Wallet's settlement policy.
