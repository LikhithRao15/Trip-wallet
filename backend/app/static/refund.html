Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026 | Effective Date: September 16, 2026

At Trip Wallet, we strive to maintain transparent, straightforward financial operations for all group trip participants. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines how payments, cancellations, and wallet reconciliations are handled when using our platform.

Summary: All online funding is processed through Razorpay. Failed transactions with debited funds are automatically refunded to the original payment source. Trip pool contributions that have been credited to an active trip are governed by the trip's expense split and final settlement calculations.

1. Failed or Incomplete Payment Transactions

If your bank account or payment method is debited during a wallet funding attempt, but the transaction fails to complete, is timed out, or does not successfully verify on our servers:

  • The trip wallet balance will not be credited.
  • The payment gateway (Razorpay) and your issuing bank initiate an automatic refund back to your source account (UPI, credit/debit card, or bank account).
  • Reversal timelines typically range between 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank's operational cycle.
  • If the debited amount is not restored after 7 business days, please share your Razorpay Payment ID with our support team at support@tripwallet.example.com for investigation.

2. Cancellation of Trip Wallet Contributions

Trip Wallet allows members to pool money for group travel expenses. Due to the collaborative nature of shared trip purses:

  • Once a payment has been captured and successfully credited to a trip wallet, it cannot be canceled autonomously through an instant in-app chargeback, because the pooled balance may already have been drawn down for trip expenses (e.g., hotel deposits, transit bookings).
  • If a member wishes to withdraw their contribution before any trip expenses are incurred, the trip administrator must coordinate the withdrawal or record an appropriate adjustment within the trip ledger.

3. Trip Conclusion & Balance Settlement

When a trip finishes, the trip administrator marks the trip as completed or settled:

  • The platform generates a final settlement matrix showing each participant's total contributions minus their allocated share of trip expenses.
  • Members with excess contributions (where contribution exceeds expense share) are identified as creditors entitled to return of their unspent funds.
  • Members who contributed less than their share of expenses are debtors owing reimbursement to the group.
  • Final redistribution of unused funds is executed per the trip group's settlement agreement and recorded within the trip settlement dashboard.

4. Duplicate Payments

If a technical glitch or accidental double-click causes duplicate transactions for the same planned contribution:

  • Notify our support team within 48 hours of the transaction.
  • Provide your registered phone/email, Trip ID, and both Razorpay Payment IDs.
  • Upon verification of duplicate capture, our support team will process a refund for the duplicate transaction back to the original payment instrument via Razorpay within 5–7 business days.

5. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you believe an unauthorized payment was made from your payment instrument:

  • Please immediately contact our support team at support@tripwallet.example.com to secure your account and trace the payment metadata.
  • We cooperate fully with payment aggregators, partner banks, and law enforcement in accordance with applicable regulations.

6. Policy Updates

Trip Wallet reserves the right to modify or amend this Refund and Cancellation Policy at any time. Changes become effective immediately upon posting to this URL.
